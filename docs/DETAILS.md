# Pixie Privacy Pro — in detail

[Русская версия](DETAILS.ru.md)

- The second tray icon (a crosshair) switches the PIXY hardware auto-tracking with one click. Blue — on and confirmed by the camera; grey — off or unavailable in privacy; yellow — waiting for video or unknown state (see the tooltip). The same buttons are in the tray menu and the main window. Windows may put the new icon in the hidden tray area.
- In privacy, auto-tracking never opens the lens. The last confirmed or chosen mode is stored in HKCU\Software\PixiePrivacyPro\Tracking and restored when Pro leaves privacy. While waiting, the camera reports only state 3: the chosen tracking is confirmed once video starts. If another program changes the active mode, the icon and the stored preference follow it.

- A click on the tray icon switches the PIXY's own privacy mode. Green lock — privacy; red eye — the lens is open, including waiting; grey icon — unknown state. An open camera does not prove that anyone is watching or recording.
- The state is polled once a second. Leaving privacy outside our button shows a warning at the top centre of the main monitor: a burning eye and "I see you", without a background, over all windows. The eye fades soon after the sound (about 4 seconds without sound) and never takes clicks or focus. Which apps use the camera is shown in the window and the journal. The same warning appears if the camera is already open when Pro starts. A very short change between two polls can be missed. The monitor works while Pro is running.
- Apps that use the camera and microphone right now are shown in the window. The source is Windows' access records (ConsentStore in HKCU): they cover any camera, not only the PIXY, and do not see apps reading the Pixie virtual camera.
- A right click on the tray icon opens the control center; the classic menu is Shift + right click: frequent actions on top (privacy, tracking, window), all options in the "Settings" submenu, then "Event log" and "Exit". The "Test" button on the "Warning" page shows the eye with its sound.
- Event log: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PixiePrivacyPro\events.log` (tray menu "Event log"). It records privacy changes, apps starting and stopping to use the camera and microphone, lost connection to the camera and changes of the microphone protection. Over 1 MB the log moves to `events.log.1`.
- If the camera does not answer over USB for more than 10 seconds (for example, EMEET Studio holds it), a notification says that privacy changes are not tracked meanwhile. Stopping the microphone protection service or turning the microphone on also shows a notification.
- The microphone of the physical PIXY is disabled as a Windows recording device, not just muted. The PixiePrivacyProMicGuard service checks it every 3 seconds and disables it again when needed, regardless of the window and of sign-in. It is identified by VID 328F / PID 00C0 / audio interface MI_02; other microphones are never changed. A new USB port may take a moment to be picked up. This is software protection, not a physical switch: the microphone stays off until you, or a program running under your account, turn it back on.
- Preview with 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 and circle frames. The "Mirrored" switch under the preview turns on the mirror in the camera itself: every program and the preview see it. The frame can be dragged with the mouse; the slider sets its size. The setting is kept in the user profile.
- The "Pixie Privacy Pro Camera" virtual camera passes only the chosen part. Pick it in your call or recording app instead of the physical EMEET PIXY. Square and 4:3 are fitted into the 1280×720 output with black bars and no stretching. The circle is cut from the square on a black background.
- On Windows 11, Pro also creates the Media Foundation camera "Pixie Privacy Pro Camera (MF)". Windows lists it as "Pixie Privacy Pro Camera (MF) (Windows Virtual Camera)". Apps that do not see DirectShow cameras see it: the Camera app, UWP apps, new video clients. The camera exists while Pro runs and shows the same cropped frame. If Pro sends no frames for more than a second, it shows black. It is switched off in the tray menu ("Camera for Windows 11 apps"). The video source (`PixieVirtualCamera.dll`) is loaded by the Windows Camera Frame Server service; frames come through the shared memory `Global\PixiePrivacyProCamera`.
- Capture starts by itself when an app reads "Pixie Privacy Pro Camera" (DirectShow or MF): there is no need to open the window or the preview. When the app stops reading (after 5 seconds), capture stops. While the camera is in privacy, not answering or starting, the app gets a placeholder instead of black: a green closed lock without text (apps often mirror the picture). While the camera starts, the app keeps seeing the placeholder until the first frame. The yellow open lock (camera starting) and the grey one (camera not answering) appear only in Pro's window, under "What apps see". Instead of the green lock you can show your own picture: "Privacy picture" in the window ("Choose own picture…", "Restore lock") or in the tray menu. The picture is fitted into 1280×720 without cropping; its copy is kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PixiePrivacyPro\privacy-image.png`. After leaving privacy (tray icon, window button) video returns to the app by itself.
- "When an app opens the Pixie camera" (window and tray menu):
  - "Show placeholder" (default, safer): the app sees the placeholder (the lock or your picture); you open the lens yourself with a click on the tray icon.
  - "Open right away": as soon as an app starts reading the Pixie camera, Pro opens the lens and passes video. "Trusted apps" limits this: only they open the lens by themselves, the others see the placeholder (and a notification appears). An empty list means any program. Apps are recognised by file name. Pro finds out who reads the camera like this: DirectShow programs — by the thread of the Unity Capture filter, which runs in their process only while reading (programs that merely list cameras do not count); MF camera programs — by Windows' camera access records (they do not tell cameras apart, so a program using another camera at the same time is listed too). If you turn privacy on while an app reads the camera, it stays on until the camera is opened next time.
- The PIXY powers up with the lens open (computer start, wake from sleep, replug). The PixiePrivacyProMicGuard service turns privacy on as soon as the camera appears, even before sign-in; Pro also closes an open lens at start without a warning if nobody uses the camera (with "Close the lens when nobody uses the Pixie camera" on).
- The "Camera and microphone" group (and "Settings → Camera and microphone" in the tray) changes settings stored in the camera itself; they work in every program:
  - "Camera timer": never / 10 s / 30 s / 1 min / 5 min / 15 min. The camera lowers the lens by itself when there is no video and nobody controls it over USB, also without Pixie Pro. The timer does not fire during video. The first start sets 1 minute. Any USB request restarts this timer, so Pro checks an open camera without video less often than the timer fires.
  - "Camera microphone": "Fully disabled" (default; the service disables the PIXY microphone in Windows, so apps cannot record from it) or on with "Noise reduction", "Live sound" or "Original" processing. The choice is kept in HKLM\SOFTWARE\PixiePrivacyPro\Microphone (the service lets users change it) and the service applies it within 3 seconds.
  - "Gesture control", "Mirrored in the camera", "Flip image".
- The hotkey switches privacy from any program. The default is Win+Alt+Space (easy with the left hand alone); in the window you pick the modifiers (Win+Alt, Ctrl+Alt, Ctrl+Shift, Win+Shift, Ctrl+Win) and the third key (Space, A–Z, 0–9, F1–F12). If Windows or another program holds the combination, Pro says so.
- After leaving privacy, apps see the placeholder for 2 more seconds while the lens rises and the brightness settles.
- Pro keeps one USB connection to the camera (faster switching) and says so plainly if EMEET Studio holds the camera.
- "Close the lens on lock, sleep and screen off" (on by default): Win+L, session disconnect, sleep and monitor off turn privacy on and stop the video. The lens does not open again by itself when you come back.
- "Warning sound" (off by default) adds a sound to the "I see you" eye. Under "Which sound": "Standard: drone and voice" (a synthesized drone and a phrase in a Windows voice), "Standard: ominous voice" (dark-voice.mp3 from the program folder) or "Own sound" (MP3/WAV/WMA, copied to %LOCALAPPDATA%\PixiePrivacyPro). The ▶ button shows the eye and plays the chosen sound.
- The tray tooltip shows the state, how long the lens has been open and who reads the Pixie camera (or who uses a camera according to Windows); in privacy — who sees the placeholder. The same names are in the usage line of the window and in the journal.
- "Close the lens when nobody uses the Pixie camera" (on by default): privacy returns when apps stop reading the camera and the preview is closed, and when Pro exits. If someone else opened the lens (for example Windows Settings → Cameras, or a program reading the PIXY directly) and nobody uses a camera for 15 seconds — neither the Pixie camera nor any camera according to Windows — privacy returns too. The same applies to a lens opened from the tray icon if no program starts reading within 15 seconds.
- The "Open camera and preview" button is only for looking at the picture and setting the frame; it takes the camera out of privacy. Closing the window does not stop video to apps.
- Some apps (for example TikTok LIVE Studio with "Keep always active") read the camera all the time while running: capture and the lens then stay open until you turn privacy on.
